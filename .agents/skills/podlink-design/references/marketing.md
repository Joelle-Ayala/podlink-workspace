# Marketing surfaces

Use with `web/DESIGN.md`.

## Goal

Marketing should make a real Podlink product truth feel inevitable, not decorate a feature list.

## Before composing

State:

- who the page is for,
- what they currently believe/do,
- what they must believe by the end,
- the single primary action,
- the real product/media asset that can prove the promise.

## Choose a grammar

Prefer one of the Podlink page grammars rather than a default SaaS sequence.

### Editorial Argument
Best when the visitor must first understand the problem differently.

### Product Reveal
Best when the product itself is the proof.

### Working Surface
Best for `/analyze` and other tools. Let the visitor act quickly.

### Proof Story
Best for case studies/work.

### Conversion Utility
Best for services/ICP pages.

## Composition rules

- Use real product/media as the strongest visual whenever possible.
- Do not alternate section backgrounds mechanically.
- Avoid consecutive card grids.
- Typography and whitespace should do meaningful layout work.
- Keep one visual peak per major acquisition page.
- One primary action per decision region.
- Place proof near the claim it substantiates.
- Large claims need concrete visual/product evidence nearby.
- Do not reveal every capability at once; a page should have a story, not an inventory dump.

## Product presentation

A product visual should answer a question:

- What will I see?
- What will I know?
- What becomes easier?
- What does this replace?

Crop/scale to the answer. A full dashboard shrunk until illegible is not proof.

Use consistent framing but vary composition around the frame.

## Signature interaction

Optional for major acquisition pages.

A good signature interaction:

- communicates product meaning,
- is understandable when static,
- is unique to the page's story,
- does not block content or navigation.

Example: one episode visually resolving into audio + YouTube + derivative assets + a unified report.

A bad signature interaction is generic parallax or floating decoration.

## Proof

Prefer:

- real screenshots,
- real show art,
- real public episodes,
- real clips,
- cleared case-study metrics,
- externally checkable links.

Never invent a fake dashboard number for composition.

## Mobile

Recompose intentionally:

- crop the product differently,
- choose the order of copy/product,
- remove nonessential ambient layers,
- preserve the primary action without crowding,
- avoid tiny desktop screenshots.

## Final question

If the page lost all decorative effects, would the hierarchy, product proof and story still work?

If not, fix the composition before adding more styling.
