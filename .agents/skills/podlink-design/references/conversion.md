# Conversion and high-intent pages

Use for services, founders, PR partners, agencies/networks and other ICP-specific pages.

## Page job

A high-intent page should help a qualified visitor decide whether Podlink is relevant and what to do next.

It should not mimic the homepage.

## Default grammar

Use Conversion Utility:

problem → qualification → offer/process → proof → commercial detail → action

## CTA hierarchy

- self-serve product pages lead to product actions,
- high-ticket service pages lead to human/contact actions,
- partner pages may lead to a pilot/conversation,
- one primary CTA per decision region.

Use concrete labels:

- Analyze your podcast
- See my podcast opportunities
- Explore a partner pilot
- Book a call

Avoid vague “Learn more” as the primary action.

## Proof proximity

Place proof close to the claim it substantiates.

Examples:

- guesting promise → actual live placements,
- content-repurposing promise → actual clips,
- production promise → show/media proof,
- outcome claim → cleared case-study data.

A logo strip alone is weaker than a relevant artifact.

## Qualification

Good conversion pages are willing to say who the offer is for and who it is not for.

Qualification reduces low-fit calls and increases trust.

## Pricing/commercial detail

Use the most specific truthful detail available.

Do not hide known entry pricing behind decorative UI merely to create mystery.

Do not publish unapproved/legacy pricing because it exists in old documents.

## Services + software

When appropriate, show the connection:

DIY software → DWY assistance → DFY service.

Do not make services feel like an unrelated agency bolted onto Podlink.

## Partner/white-label pages

Clarify:

- who owns the client relationship,
- what Podlink fulfills,
- how reporting/proof works,
- brand/white-label expectations,
- pilot or commitment model.

Do not imply cleared wholesale clients if names are not cleared.

## Copy density

High-intent visitors tolerate more detail than top-of-funnel visitors, but every section must advance the decision.

Cut:

- generic benefits,
- repeated process explanations,
- decorative stats,
- proof irrelevant to the ICP.
