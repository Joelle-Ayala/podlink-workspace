## The idea
One linkable, defensible study answering the question every podcaster secretly asks:
"how many downloads until my show can make money?" — the Monetization Line. It anchors
Podlink's authority position (we're the people who publish real numbers with sources) and is
the natural lead asset for /blog or /guides when those routes open (sitemap plan marks them
"later").

## Data we can stand on
1. **OP3 public data** — open, independently operated, queryable. The download-percentile
   framing already referenced in the sitemap plan (26 / 72 / 231 / 539 / 3,062 downloads in
   7 days for p25/p50/p75/p90/p99) — VERIFY against current OP3 published stats before use;
   cite OP3's own publication, with date.
2. **Podcast Index dump** — universe sizing (see producer-universe-count.md), episode cadence.
3. **Sponsorship market rates** — the pricing-SLA decision sheet's 2026 market research
   (rev-share 20–40% market range) + published CPM benchmarks (VERIFY: find 2026 primary
   sources; marketing decks are NOT sources).

## The line, computed not asserted
Monetization Line = downloads/episode where (CPM_low × downloads × spots/episode) clears a
floor worth the effort (e.g. $100/episode). Present as a range under stated CPM assumptions,
each assumption sourced. Overlay the percentile table → "what percent of active shows are
above the line" falls out arithmetically.
