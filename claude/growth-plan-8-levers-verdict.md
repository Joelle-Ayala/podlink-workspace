## Unit-math stress test (the decisive input)
$10k MRR = **345 Pro units at sticker** ($517 at annual-effective $19.33/mo — the 8×
annual discount is a real MRR haircut). The $40–60 blended-ARPA route requires 16–44% of
customers on Studio — **which cannot be purchased** (schema block). Months-1–2 target of
50–80 paid needs 100k–390k sends against ~5–15k warmup-safe capacity on the 2 planned
domains (**7–25× short**); 2–4k sends/day requires ~20–45 warmed domains / 40–130 inboxes
— month 2–3 infrastructure at the earliest. At canon GRR (~35% ⇒ ~8.4%/mo churn), netting
+345 units in 6 months needs ~71 gross adds/month ≈ 350k sends/month. Concierge at 45
min/user breaches the <3-month payback rule below ~49–78% trial conversion.
**Corrected trajectory: $2–3k MRR by month 6 is the defensible target; $10k lands month
15–24, contingent on Studio shipping and GRR proving out well above 35%.** The plan's
shape survives; its calendar and ARPA do not.
