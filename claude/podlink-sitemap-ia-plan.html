Information architecture

How services change the Podlink sitemap

Adding six services pages doesn't just add URLs — it puts a second business on the domain. This is the surface map, the collision risk, and the order to ship it in.

The bigger picture

Four surfaces, one brand

Services land on podlink.ai, but the sitemap question spans every host. Two of these are actively working against you today.

podlink.ai

The marketing site. Next.js on Vercel. Product pages today, product + services after this. This is the only host that should rank for anything commercial.

app.podlink.ai

The product. MagicAI on Railway — but it currently also serves a complete duplicate marketing site at / and /features. Two public marketing sites with near-identical copy. Decide before DNS cutover, not after.

podlink.fm

Creator link-in-bio pages. Root 302s to podlink.ai. /{handle} pages and /directory still serve. That directory is a genuine long-tail SEO asset nobody is using yet.

checkout.podlink.ai

Checkout only, not started. The storefront lives on podlink.ai — but Shopify hosts checkout itself and it cannot run on Vercel, so one Shopify-owned hostname is required. Brand it; never expose *.myshopify.com.

The real risk

Features and services will fight each other

Six of eight feature pages target keywords the services pages also want. Left alone, Google picks one, splits the authority, and you rank for neither. The fix is intent separation written into the titles — tool language on features, done-for-you language on services.

Feature pageServices pageRiskSeparation
/features/clips-and-social /services/podcast-clips High Feature → “AI podcast clip generator”. Service → “podcast clip editing service”.

/features/show-notes		 /services/podcast-editing High Feature → “automatic transcripts / AI show notes”. Service → “podcast editing & production service”.
/features/download-analytics /services/podcast-growth Medium Feature → “podcast analytics dashboard”. Service → “podcast audience growth agency”.
/features/newsletter /services/podcast-growth Medium Feature owns the tool. Service mentions newsletter only as a deliverable.
/features/link-in-bio — Medium Competes with podlink.fm/{handle}, not with services. Separate problem.
/features/templates
/features/multilingual		 — None Product-only. Leave alone.

Which side should lead? The services pages. Product pages compete against Descript, Riverside and Buzzsprout for head terms — brutal and expensive. Services compete for “podcast booking agency”, “podcast editing service”, “podcast ad agency” — winnable, higher intent, and worth 50–200× more per conversion. Services should carry the indexing priority; features should convert traffic that's already on-site.

Order of operations

What to do, in sequence

  1. Unblock Vercel. Nothing on this page ships until the repo access is granted. Still the top of the list.
  2. Build /contact. Six pages of CTAs currently point at a 404.
  3. Merge the services bundle and extend sitemap.ts to derive from the services list as well as features.
  4. Rewrite the six colliding feature titles toward tool intent. Twenty minutes of work that protects both sides.
  5. Kill the duplicate marketing site on app.podlink.ai before DNS cutover.
  6. Build the case-study tier. Unblocked — permission granted across the board.
  7. Verify the CVS and Oracle figures, or drop them permanently. Those two are a factual question, not a permission one.
  8. Confirm 2026 pricing, then drop the noindex and let services into the index.
