## 0. The context that was missing

Podlink is not a new venture with a blank history. It is the podcast half of **Minting House**, which is splitting into two companies:

- **Podlink** — everything podcast: editing, clips, advertising, guest booking, growth. Plus the podlink.ai SaaS product.

## 10. Delivery process, as actually practiced

Reconstructed from real Slack conversation. This closes the SLA gap — not with a
written policy, because none exists, but with the operating cadence of a show
that ships every week.

**Team:** host records and writes descriptions/tags · editor cuts the episode and
every clip and writes captions · growth ops handles distribution, paid launch,
monetization, analytics and SEO · a fourth person sells sponsorship.

**Weekly cycle:** Monday team meeting → episode drops **Friday, 10:00–10:30am PT**.

1. **T-1:** editor delivers rough cut + full transcript for pre-flight.
2. **Pre-flight compliance check** — transcript screened for profanity in the
   first 30–60 seconds and for language in titles/tags that throttles YouTube
   reach or gets the paid ad disapproved. This is a real, learned discipline:
   one episode's ad was disapproved for "shocking content" and required a
   timestamped edit list and re-upload.
3. **Drop**, with a stated target audience for the episode.
4. **Same day:** paid launch submitted, capped at $200, paused once organic
   recommendations take over.
5. **Same day:** launch clip posted with caption and hashtags, mirrored to X,
   LinkedIn and Facebook.
6. **T+2 to T+14:** 2–4 further clips drip-fed, each with its own caption.
7. **Monetization:** ad-break markers set in Megaphone per episode.
8. **Underperformance loop:** transcript + retention report analyzed, diagnosis
   returned. A real one: *"the 30-second retention cliff — a massive drop-off in
   the first 45 seconds"* → fix was trimming the opening.
9. **Reporting:** biweekly per-channel sheet — last 5 episodes by channel,
   averages, last 30 days.

Episodes run long (1.5–3 hours). Newsletter goes out per episode on Beehiiv.
29 static episode pages were rebuilt with optimized titles, meta descriptions and
301 redirects.

**This is the delivery proof the editing and clips pages needed.** It's also the
basis for the turnaround answers now on those pages — both carry `TODO(sla)`
markers because committing to them contractually is Joelle's call.
