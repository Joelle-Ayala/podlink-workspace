## 1. Podcast editing / production

**Anchor:** $275/episode; $750/mo for 4 episodes produced (note: the bundle implies $187.50/ep — below the per-episode anchor; fix this inconsistency either way).

Audio+writing: $900–1,640/mo | 48h audio-only; 72h standard; 14-day money-back; revisions not stated | Mid-market subscription editing, dedicated editor, no contracts |
| [Resonate Recordings](https://resonaterecordings.com/pricing) | Not published (custom after discovery call). (3P, [Castos roundup](https://castos.com/best-podcast-editing-services/)): tiers cited from $59–$199+/ep | Launch program 60–90 days to first episode; per-episode TAT not published | Full-service B2B production + launch strategy |

| [The Podcast Consultant](https://thepodcastconsultant.com/blog/podcast-editing-services) | (3P, Castos roundup): from $250/ep; $499–$999/mo. Own blog benchmarks: mid freelance $150–350/ep, mid-agency $1,500–3,000/mo | Cites 3–5 business days standard; rush 24–48h at +20–50% | B2B/finance specialist, editorial quality |
| [Podcast Engineers](https://www.podcastengineers.com/blogs/best-podcast-editing-services-2026/) | (3P, Castos roundup): $199–$499/mo by count/length | 3–5 days | Budget-to-mid managed editing |
| Freedom Podcasting / ScrubCast (3P, [Castos roundup](https://castos.com/best-podcast-editing-services/)) | $85–$255/ep; ScrubCast $120/ep or $440/mo (4 eps) | Freedom: 3 days | Value per-episode editors |

**Position vs market:** $275/ep is **mid** — above subscription shops ($110–200/ep effective), below B2B specialists ($350–600/ep effective). The $750/mo bundle is **low-mid** vs We Edit Podcasts' $800/mo for audio-only.

**Recommendation:** Keep **from $275/ep**; raise the 4-episode bundle to **$950/mo** (~$237/ep, still under the $250–350 mid-freelance band while bundling production extras). Market benchmark for editing cost: [Rephonic](https://rephonic.com/blog/podcast-editing-cost/), [Cleanvoice](https://cleanvoice.ai/blog/podcast-editing-rates/).

**SLA:** Market standard is 3–5 business days; the aggressive players commit 48–72h ([We Edit Podcasts](https://www.weeditpodcasts.com/pricing/), [Castos](https://castos.com/productions/) 3 days audio). Podlink already runs a weekly Friday-drop cycle with day-before rough cuts, so publishing **"3 business days standard; rough cut guaranteed 24h before your scheduled drop"** is at or better than market and already operationally proven. Add "rush 48h at +25%" as upsell (market charges +20–50%).

| [Solvis Media](https://solvismedia.com/podcast-editing-for-video-and-audio/) | Starter from $1,750 (4 episodes incl. full edit + 3 clips each ≈ 12 clips) | Audio 72h; clips within a week; 1 revision/video | Regional full-service video studio |
| [Komet Media benchmark](https://www.kometmedia.com/blogs/podcast-cost) | Short-form clips (3–5/ep): $300–$800/mo line item; freelance video-ep-with-clips $300–700/ep | not found | Cost-guide benchmark |
| AI floor: [OpusClip](https://fluxnote.io/guides/opus-clip-pricing-2026) | Free tier; Starter $15/mo; Pro $29/mo (credit-based) | Minutes (automated) | AI auto-clipping — the price floor buyers compare against |

**Position vs market:** $150–175/clip is **high**. AI tools compressed the floor to ~$0; human subscription services deliver at ~$30–125/clip effective. Premium "strategy + human" clipping survives, but nobody with published prices is at $175/clip in volume.

**Recommendation:** Reposition to a monthly package — **from $995/mo for 8–10 platform-native clips** (~$100–125/clip) with strategy/selects (Trueframe-style positioning), and stop quoting $150+/clip. Keep a from-$95/clip a-la-carte rate for overflow only.

**SLA:** Market: VidChops guarantees 1–2 days; Solvis "within a week." **Publish "clips delivered within 3 business days of the final episode cut, 1 revision round included"** — comfortably deliverable on a weekly cycle and beats the average.

**Position vs market:** $399/booked is **low-mid** on an effective per-booking basis ($300–1,000 market) and the pay-per-placement model is a genuine differentiator (most competitors charge retainers whether or not they place you). **Recommendation:** **$750 setup + $449 per booked appearance** — modest lift that stays under the market's effective midpoint while the performance model does the selling; keep a flagship campaign at $7,500+.

**SLA:** Market norm is **no hard guarantee**; typical promised cadence is 2–4 bookings/mo with first placements in 30–45 days. Podlink can differentiate with **"first confirmed booking within 30 days of kickoff or that month's setup fee credited"** — low risk since fees are per-placement anyway.

| General agency norm ([Clicksgeek growth pricing guide](https://clicksgeek.com/growth-marketing-agency-pricing/)) | ~10–20% of ad spend or flat retainer is the standard paid-media model | Retainers commonly $1.5K–$10K/mo | Cross-channel benchmark |

**Position vs market:** Podcast-specific management fees are almost universally **not published**. Against the general 10–20%-of-spend norm, $1,850/mo flat is **low-mid** — at a $10K/mo spend it equals 18.5%, but it stops scaling above that, underpricing larger accounts.

**Recommendation:** **From $2,500/mo or 15% of spend, whichever is greater, with a $7,500/mo minimum spend** — matches the published test-budget floor ($5–10K) and stops flat-fee leakage on bigger budgets.

**SLA:** No published SLAs found in this category ("not found" is the honest market answer). Practical norm is 2–4 weeks from signed IO to first ad live. Recommend publishing **"campaign live within 21 days of creative approval; standardized monthly attribution report"** — a differentiator precisely because nobody else commits.

## 5. Podcast sponsorship sales representation (creator side)

**Anchor:** 15% of revenue + $350 setup.

| Provider | Rev share | Minimum audience | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| [True Native Media](https://truenativemedia.com/faq-sections/) | **30%** baked-in / **40%** dynamically inserted | 10,000 downloads/ep (embedded); 100,000/mo (DAI) | 18-month exclusive contracts |
| [Libsyn Ads (AdvertiseCast)](https://advertising.libsyn.com/podcast-advertising-faq-for-podcaster) | **30%** ("sell a spot for $100, we keep $30") | Recommends 10+ episodes and ~20,000 listeners/ep; smaller shows routed to Auto Ads | Marketplace |
| Gumball (via [Buzzsprout](https://www.buzzsprout.com/blog/get-podcast-sponsors)) | **20%** | not published | Self-serve host-read marketplace |
| Podcorn (via [Buzzsprout](https://www.buzzsprout.com/blog/get-podcast-sponsors)) | **10%** | none stated | Self-serve, creator does the selling |
| Acast, Podbean (via Buzzsprout) | Not disclosed | Not disclosed | Network monetization programs |

**Position vs market:** 15% is **well below market** for full-service representation (20–40%); only self-serve marketplaces where the creator does the work go lower. Podlink is leaving 5–15 points on the table while doing rep-level work.

**Recommendation:** **20% of revenue, drop the $350 setup fee** — still undercuts every full-service rep by 10+ points, and killing the setup fee removes signup friction that percent-only marketplaces don't have. Set eligibility at **5,000 downloads/ep** (half of True Native's bar = clear positioning for mid-size shows the big reps reject).

**SLA:** No rep publishes an SLA (not found). Differentiate with **"first sponsor pitch-out within 21 days of onboarding, quarterly revenue reporting, and 6-month (not 18-month) exclusivity."**

## Cross-cutting notes

- **Transparency is the arbitrage:** in categories 3–6, almost no competitor publishes prices or SLAs. Publishing real numbers + real commitments is itself a positioning move for the relaunch.
- **Verify before print:** all figures marked (3P) — Interview Valet $1,375/mo, Kitcaster, Resonate tiers, The Podcast Consultant $250/ep — come from 2026 third-party roundups ([Talks.co](https://talks.co/p/podcast-booking-services/), [Castos](https://castos.com/best-podcast-editing-services/)), not the providers' own sites.
- **Not found / not verifiable:** podcast-specific ad-management fee percentages (category 4), Guestio agency pricing, Trueframe pricing, Kitcaster current packages, any published SLA in categories 4–6.
- **Fix the editing bundle math:** $750/mo ÷ 4 eps = $187.50/ep vs the $275/ep anchor; the recommended $950/mo (≈$237/ep) narrows the gap while keeping bundle incentive.
