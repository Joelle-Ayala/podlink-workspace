# Website and Visual Asset Reuse Brief

## Objective

Launch `podlink.ai` and reskin the app using existing MagicAI and Biolink assets/templates wherever possible, instead of designing from scratch.

Assumption: founder can legally acquire/use purchased MagicAI and Biolink assets/templates as source material.

## Core principle

Use MagicAI/Biolink as a visual/component/template library, not as final brand identity.

Reuse:

- layouts
- components
- icons
- cards
- pricing tables
- dashboards
- public page templates
- demo structures
- visual patterns

Replace or regenerate:

- logos
- favicons
- vendor-branded screenshots
- generic AI screenshots
- irrelevant feature visuals
- testimonials/reviews from vendors
- anything that visibly says MagicAI, Magic Project, Biolink, 66biolinks, CodeCanyon, or vendor names

## Page positioning

### Homepage

Positioning:

> The podcast growth workspace for every episode.

Subheadline:

> Create your podcast page, turn long episodes into clips, generate captions and promo content, and publish faster from one dashboard.

Primary CTA:

> Create your free Podlink

### Podlink Page

> A podcast-first link page for your show, episodes, clips, sponsors, and socials.

### AI Clip Studio

> Turn long podcast episodes into short-form clips for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.

### Episode Content Kit

> Generate the copy every episode needs: show notes, YouTube descriptions, newsletters, social captions, and more.

### Social Publishing

> Schedule and publish episode content from the same place you create it.

### Analytics Coming Soon

> Soon: automated episode and channel reports for podcasters.
