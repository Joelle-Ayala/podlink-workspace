# Podlink Product Brief

## Product thesis

Podlink is a podcast growth workspace that helps podcasters create a public podcast page, turn episodes into clips, generate promo content, publish faster, and later prove performance through automated episode/channel reports.

## Primary users

- independent podcasters
- video podcasters
- podcast producers
- creator agencies
- podcast networks
- creators selling sponsorships
- teams creating episode recap reports manually today

## Core problem

Podcasters publish content across too many surfaces:

- podcast hosting/audio
- Spotify
- Apple
- YouTube
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels
- TikTok
- LinkedIn/X/Facebook
- public links
- sponsor CTAs

They need help creating content from episodes and, later, proving performance across channels.

## MVP wedge

Launch with existing capabilities:

1. Public podcast page at `podlink.fm/{handle}`
2. AI Episode Content Kit
3. AI Clip Studio if MagicAI Viral Clips/Captions/Video Editor are installed
4. Social publishing if MagicAI social tooling is installed and working

## Later differentiation

Automated podcast analytics:

- episode reports
- channel reports
- date filters
- platform filters
- clip attribution
- sponsor-ready reports

Analytics should be architected from day one but not promised as live until implemented.

## Product narrative

```text
Create your podcast page.
Turn long episodes into short-form clips.
Generate captions, show notes, newsletters, and social posts.
Publish faster.
Coming soon: automated episode and channel reports.
```

## Domain model

```text
podlink.ai         = marketing site
app.podlink.ai     = user dashboard/workspace
podlink.fm/{handle}= public page
```
