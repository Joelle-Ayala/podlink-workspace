## 1. The register, derived from how she actually talks
Evidence: her real messages. "lets keep everything moving forward" ·
"move things forward that you dont need me for" · "my bad, ssd was not
connected" · "adopt v2 and help me catch anything we missed" · "I'd not just
do email for now" · "check this" · "lets do it" · "is the site updated?"

**Principles:**
1. **Operator first-person.** She writes like someone running the thing, not
   observing it. "I booked", "I cut", "we shipped" — never "one should".
2. **Momentum grammar.** Short imperatives, quick pivots, corrections owned
   in half a sentence and moved past ("my bad — fixed, next"). Lowercase
   starts and standing typos are FINE on X/casual posts; clean case for
   LinkedIn long-form.
3. **Numbers as plot points, not trophies.** A number appears where the story
   turns: "the 1am slot nobody wanted" · "165 episodes" · "the clips became
   the ads." Never a brag-wall of stats.
4. **Era-specific insider credibility.** The pre-AI mechanics are the flex:
   cutting highlights by hand at the sports networks, cold email as a craft,
   the flywheel plumbing at Wolf's Den. Specificity IS the credibility —
   "regional satellite feeds" beats "worked in TV".
5. **Candid asides as values.** One dry line that shows judgment without
   preaching. The archetype: "I turned down Andrew Tate." (Gated on verbatim
   confirmation before first use.)
6. **Build-in-public practicality.** Show the working: what she shipped, what
   broke, what the honest number was. Small real numbers said plainly beat
   borrowed big ones (voice-guide §4 rule applies to her feed too).
7. **Asks are direct.** CTAs read like her DMs: "comment PODLINK and I'll
   send you the setup doc." No fake scarcity, no "🚀".

**Don't:** hustle-culture cosplay · humble-brags ("blessed to announce") ·
engagement-bait questions she wouldn't actually ask · threads that
re-explain podcasting 101 · any banned word from voice-guide §3 ·
unverified numbers (§5 gates apply to her feed EXACTLY as to the site).

## 2. Phrasing patterns (templates, not scripts)
- Cold open on the era: "In 20XX my job was [absurdly manual version of what
  PodLink now automates]." → mechanism → "that's why [feature] works the way
  it does."
- The flywheel telling: "The clips weren't marketing for the show. They were
  the ads. The ads paid for the growth. That loop is the whole company."
- The correction post: "I was wrong about [X]. Here's the number that
  changed my mind." (her fast-ownership register, productized)
- The receipts post: "[N] visitors, [N] signups, $0 spent. Chart below,
  universe stated." (Rondot format; GA4/Stripe sourced only)
- The judgment aside: one sentence, no elaboration, next paragraph moves on.
