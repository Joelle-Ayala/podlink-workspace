# Branding Voice Interview — for Joelle, by voice memo (2026-08-25)
**Purpose:** close the voice guide's three TODOs and give the AI copy customization her
actual register. Answer by voice memo, rambling encouraged — verbatim beats polished.
Skip any question; half-answers are useful. Target: 15–20 minutes total.

## 1. Origin (the war story only you can tell)
- How did the podcast work actually start? First client, first booking, what the manual
  process looked like week to week (the Apple related-shows → Snov era — tell it as a story).
- What did you do at Megaphone-era scale that a solo podcaster never sees?
- Why build Podlink instead of just keeping the agency?

## 2. Why clients hired you (their words through your memory)
- Think of MDS, MMO, Elmo, one B2B founder: what did each SAY they needed when they first
  reached out? Phrases you remember verbatim.
- What did they say after it worked? (Renewal conversations, thank-yous, referrals.)
- What did a client say when it DIDN'T work or almost churned?

## 3. Your opinions on podcasting norms (positions, not descriptions)
- What does the podcast industry get wrong? What advice do podcasters keep getting that
  you'd tell them to ignore?
- Downloads: what do people misunderstand about their numbers?
- What do you think of "you need 10–20k downloads to monetize"?
- AI podcast tools: what do they actually get wrong? What's your honest take on where AI
  helps and where it's noise?
- Hosts (Buzzsprout et al.): what should they be doing that they don't?

## 4. The USPs, as you'd say them on a client call (one answer each, out loud)
- A host asks: "why should I trust your numbers over my host's dashboard?"
- "I already pay for Linktree and a repurposing tool — why you?"
- "Will the AI make my show sound generic?"
- "Which shows could you actually book me on?" (answer it the way you would live)
- "Can't I just hire a VA instead?"
- "Why would I pay before you've proven anything?" (the free-tier answer, your way)

## 5. Texture
- Three phrases you actually say all the time to clients (your verbal tics — we want them).
- One thing you'd never say / a tone you hate in marketing.
- If Podlink were a person at a dinner party, how do they talk?

**What happens with this:** patterns get extracted into voice-guide v1.1 (P3 war stories
sourced in your first person, founder-register calibration, new earned opinions for the P1
catalog). Nothing is quoted publicly without your sign-off; standing evidence rules apply.
