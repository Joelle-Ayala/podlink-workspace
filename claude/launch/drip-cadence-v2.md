## 1. Each drip = a mini-launch package
Her framing, codified: "then 'now this one's live,' and we could do a press
release, make content, a video — dates for us to stick to so we're not doing
too much at once."
Per drip, in order:
1. **Verify** — the feature works on app.podlink.ai, clicked by a human
   (existing rule, no exceptions).
2. **Changelog entry** (web/src/content/changelog.ts) — ships on verify;
   this is what makes it "live" on the site. Not gated (site update).
3. **Founder post** — drafted in founder-voice, HER edit, HER send
   (always hers; also satisfies the external-comms gate by construction).
4. **Brand post** — company surface (X/LinkedIn), product voice, drafted
   for her approval per the comms gate.
5. **Short video/content asset** — ≤40s screen capture of the real feature
   doing the real thing (asset-shot-list.md pattern; I script + shot-list,
   she records or lends a session for capture). Optional per drip, required
   for tentpoles.
6. **Optional PR** — Podnews-style pitch ONLY for genuinely-new entries
   (contact-discovery P1, Show Report, directory listing). Ordinary stock-
   feature drips don't get PR — that currency is spent sparingly.
**Spacing: 2 weeks between drips, hard floor 1 week — the calendar exists
precisely so we're "not doing too much at once."** Tentpoles interrupt.
