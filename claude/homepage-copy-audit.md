# Homepage Copy Audit — Befores/Afters (v1 draft for Joelle sign-off, 2026-08-20)
**Lens:** claude/copy-audit-brief.md (pain → objection → USP bridge, per section).
**Also resolves:** claims-truth RED rows 3/4/8 (auto-transcription, episode workspace,
present-tense clips) — all three live on this INDEXED page.
**Dual-funnel answer:** personas doc §4.1 prescribes it exactly — a two-door split above
the fold. Adopted here as a compact door-band directly under the hero (not persona-split
routing): the nav already separates the businesses; the homepage must too.

---

## 1. HERO
**BEFORE (live):** "Record the episode. Podlink does the other four hours" / "Show notes,
clips, social posts, a newsletter and download numbers you can defend — generated from the
episode you just published, on the podcast host you already use."
**FAILS:** headline is strong (real S1/S2 pain, keep). Sub: "clips" = RED row 8 (unshipped);
"generated from the episode you just published" implies automatic-on-publish (RED rows 3/4
adjacency). Beat (c) half-done: "numbers you can defend" gestures at USP 1 without the
sponsor bridge.
**AFTER:** headline unchanged. Sub: "Show notes, social posts, a newsletter and download
numbers a sponsor can check — written from what you actually said, on the podcast host you
already use." Reassurance line unchanged ("Connect your RSS feed in under a minute. No host
migration, no card required.")
*Bridge: pain (four hours) → objection (host switching, AI slop via "what you actually
said") → USP 1+2+3. Clips removed until Clip Studio ships.*

## 2. NEW — THE TWO-DOOR BAND (insert directly under hero)
**BEFORE:** absent — the homepage is product-only; services are invisible above the fold.
This is the single biggest drift: P1/P2 buyers (the revenue-primary funnel) get no door.
**AFTER (compact band, two cards):**
Door 1 — "**Software that handles post-publish.** See your downloads free, then turn every
episode into a week of marketing — in your voice. → Start free"
Door 2 — "**A team that does it for you.** Production, clips, booking, sponsorship and
growth — for people who'd rather run their business than their post-production. → Explore
services"
*Bridge: §0's "two businesses, one center of gravity"; Door 2 line is the personas doc's
services line nearly verbatim.*

## 3. WHAT-IT-DOES ("One episode in, a week of material out")
**BEFORE:** four sub-blocks; "Every episode... measured, transcribed and ready to be
repurposed" (RED row 3 implies auto-transcription); "Podlink cuts it" (RED row 8, clips).
Feature cards describe capabilities without pain anchors — beat (a) missing throughout.
**FAILS:** (a) no persona words; claims-RED ×2.
**AFTER:** open the section with the sourced market pain, then the blocks:
Lead-in: "Podcasters spend more time on descriptions, show notes and posts than on the
episode itself. That's the part Podlink takes." *(paraphrase of the sourced verbatim,
personas §2-S1)*
- "Keep publishing where you publish" block: unchanged (already the objection answer).
- "Know what actually landed": add the sponsor bridge — "numbers from OP3, not our own
  counter — so when a sponsor asks for trend data, you send a source they can check."
- "Get the writing off your evening": "transcribed and ready" → "bring the episode and its
  transcript, and the writing is done in the time it takes to make coffee."
- "Turn one episode into a week of promotion": drop "Podlink cuts it, writes it" → "Every
  episode is a week of marketing material. Podlink writes it and gives it somewhere to
  live." (clips language out until shipped; the clips feature card stays only if its own
  page copy is fixed the same way — see feature-page application).

## 4. HOW-IT-WORKS
**BEFORE step 2 (live):** "Each episode lands in a workspace of its own, transcribed on
arrival, with the show notes... drafted from what was actually said" — RED rows 3+4: no
persistence, no auto-transcription, no workspace-on-publish.
**AFTER step 2:** "Bring in an episode — paste the link or drop in the transcript — and get
the kit: show notes, titles, description, newsletter and social drafts, in your format,
because you set it once."
**Step 3 BEFORE:** fine factually (downloads per episode/app/country are real). Add the S4
bridge at the end: "— so the next episode is a decision, and the sponsor email writes
itself."
*(Step 1 unchanged — RSS + OP3 prefix is true and is the objection answer.)*

## 5. FAQ
**BEFORE:** strongest section — host-switching ✓, AI-makes-things-up ✓, sounds-like-a-bot ✓,
OP3 source ✓. Two gaps: (1) "another subscription" objection unanswered; (2) "What does it
cost" already updated to $29 but carries no consolidation bridge.
**AFTER:** merge into the cost answer: "Free to start... Pro is $29 a month — about what a
link page and a repurposer cost together, except those don't tell you which post moved
downloads." Add one FAQ: "I already pay for a link-in-bio tool and an AI writer." →
"That's the point — Pro replaces both (typically ~$48/month between them) and adds the one
thing neither sells: download numbers a sponsor can verify."
**Also fix:** FAQ "Can my producer or editor work in this with me?" currently answers with
the workspace claim (RED row 4). Rewrite: "Podlink is built for one login per show today;
producer seats and client workspaces are on the roadmap — that's the Studio tier." (Honest;
also seeds S3.)
**And:** "What is podlink.fm?" — keep; it's true and shipped.

## 6. CLOSING CTA BAND
**BEFORE:** "Your next episode could publish itself. Connect your feed, publish as normal,
and find the show notes, clips, newsletter and social copy already waiting for you."
**FAILS:** "publish itself" + "already waiting" = the automation overpromise again; "clips"
again.
**AFTER:** "Your next episode deserves the full week of attention. Connect your feed, bring
the episode, and the show notes, newsletter and social copy are done before your coffee is."
CTA buttons unchanged (Start free primary).
