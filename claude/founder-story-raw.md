## 1. The arc (as relayed)
- Podcast consumer since ~2009.
- LMU, communication studies / broadcast journalism. Created + hosted "Lion
  Fever" campus sports show; KXLU play-by-play + a 1am Saturday radio slot.
- Internships: Clear Channel / Fox Sports Radio (cutting audio highlights) ·
  Fox Sports TV production assistant (highlight clips for regional
  satellites; Best Damn Sports Show) · ESPN Radio AM 570 (Loose Cannon Show
  with Michael Thompson; some Dan Patrick work).
- Agency podcast "The Click."
- Head of growth at Happy Returns (later acquired by PayPal): booked the
  founder on e-commerce podcasts and discovered bookings drove backlinks,
  SEO, referral traffic and demos; repurposed transcripts into multimedia
  pages. ← the BOOKING thesis is born here.
- CMO for Jordan Belfort: Wolf's Den podcast — the clips-as-ads flywheel
  (clips → FB/YT ads → retargeting → free-guide funnel → products; ad
  revenue funding growth). Sponsors ~$3–8k/mo each; Oracle NetSuite ~$10k/mo
  (see §2 — handle with care). Built second show Sales School (3–5x/week,
  archive repurposing). $0 → ~$50k/mo ad revenue plus five figures/mo funnel
  revenue. Booked Ryan Holiday, Russell Brunson, Neil Patel, Eric Siu, Dave
  Asprey, Tomi Lahren. Turned down Andrew Tate. Got HBO meetings.
  ← the CLIPS-AS-GROWTH thesis is born here.
- Founded Minting House: podcast growth agency; early clipping-as-a-service;
  FIRST booking client was Dropbox (getting the DocSend founder booked
  post-acquisition); cold email as the core skill; grew full-service
  (advertising, monetization, clipping, content, booking).
- Still owns it; clients incl. JamCard, Go With Elmo, MDS, Moms Moving On.
- Now rebranding into PodLink.
