# /contact Page Template (v1 — 2026-08-20)
**Status:** stack approved for drafting. Every services CTA lands here — this page IS the
services funnel's conversion surface. MOBILE-FIRST: services buyers arrive from phones.

## Stack
1. **Header** — reframe the ask: "Tell us about your show", not "Contact us". Subhead names
   what happens (a real conversation about fit, not a sales sequence).
2. **Qualifying form** — short enough to convert, rich enough to qualify:
   show name/URL · which service (select, 6 + "not sure") · rough budget or stage (ranges).
   Nothing else. NOTE: form submission needs a backend/endpoint decision — until one exists
   the form is `TODO(contact)` and the mailto path stays primary. Never a dead form.
3. **Response-time promise** — ONLY if true/decided. No decision exists today →
   `TODO(contact): response-time promise undecided` — section omitted until Joelle sets it.
4. **Booking link** — once her URL exists (the standing `TODO(contact)` marker); mailto
   fallback stands until then.
5. **What happens next** — 3 numbered steps (call → proposal → start), mirrors the services
   template §4 timeline language.
6. **Trust strip** — cleared shows/brands, one row (services template §1 rules).

## Visual spec
Inherit v1.1 globals. Form fields: large tap targets, single column, labels above inputs,
no multi-step wizard. The page should be one thumb-scroll.

## Claims-truth tie-in
Every promise on this page is operational (response time, what-happens-next cadence) —
each needs to be TRUE as a matter of process, not product. Nothing ships un-TODO'd without
Joelle confirming the operational commitment.
